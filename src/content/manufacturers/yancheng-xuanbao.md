# Yancheng Xuanbao Environmental Technology Co., Ltd.

Environmental technology manufacturer headquartered in Yandu District,
Yancheng, Jiangsu. Profile data supplied directly by the company
(brochures, 2026-10-07).

## Company profile

Yancheng Xuanbao Environmental Technology Co., Ltd. is a high-tech
enterprise integrating R&D, production, sales and testing, focused on
industrial exhaust gas treatment. Core product lines: precious-metal
honeycomb ceramic catalysts, SCR DeNOx catalysts, CO removal catalysts,
zeolite molecular sieve adsorbents, and activated carbon products.

The company operates per QA/QC procedures and its products have been
tested and certified by domestic and international institutions including
the Xi'an Thermal Engineering Institute and the Uniper Institute.

## Corporate culture

- Vision: to become a China top-ranking and world-renowned high-tech
  environmental protection enterprise
- Mission: supplying clean air, sharing healthy life
- Values: innovation, integrity, teamwork, perseverance

## R&D center

The company has operated a technology R&D center since its founding,
with long-term cooperation with domestic universities (including Sun
Yat-sen University, Fudan University, Tongji University and the Coal
Research Institute). The laboratory holds synthesis, characterization,
modification and performance-evaluation equipment for molecular sieves,
plus BET, XRF, XRD and ICP instruments.
